# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People watching YouTube DJ sets and mixes who want to identify the current
track, browse a timestamped tracklist, and navigate playback without leaving
the video. The options page supports arranging a personal HUD layout.

## Product Purpose

Keep track information and playback controls understandable and accessible
while preserving the viewing area. Distinguish missing timing data from a
usable synchronized tracklist.

## Brand Personality

Compact, clear, consistent. This is an operating tool: reading and interaction
take priority over decoration.

## Anti-references

Avoid scattered controls, arbitrary gaps, uneven row alignment, oversized
decoration that obscures track information, and controls placed beyond easy
reach. Built-in layouts should leave space for further editing.

## Design Principles

- Group related playback actions and align their rows predictably.
- Keep track information prominent and preserve space around the layout.
- Retain user settings until an explicit save.
- Use fixed-pixel fine lines when component dimensions change.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard operation, visible focus, readable labels, supported
interface languages, and reduced-motion behavior. Do not imply a formal
accessibility certification without verification.
